# Full results: all decoders, all metrics (as of 2026-11-21, 17:18 CST)

One place for every current number. Details and history: `RESULTS.md` §7,
`results/2026-11-21_1559_p1e-3_distance_sweep.md`, `results/2026-11-21_1704_claude2_verification_and_greedy_scaling.md`.

**Conventions.** Rotated CSS surface code, Z-basis memory, **d rounds** of noisy syndrome measurement per shot,
uniform circuit-level depolarizing noise p (every gate, reset and measurement), same Stim circuits for every
decoder. Logical error rates are **per round** (from per shot: 1 − 2ε_round = (1 − 2ε_shot)^(1/d)), 95% Wilson
intervals. Code capacity = one round, perfect measurements.

**Decoders.** Ours (10): Union-Find, UF weighted, MWPM, Sparse Blossom, greedy (+2-opt), lookup, BP, BP+OSD,
BP+UF (= belief-find, Higgott et al. 2023), local BP+UF. Baselines: PyMatching, PyMatching correlated, ldpc BP+OSD.

## 1. Headlines

* **Exact matching reproduced twice:** our MWPM gives PyMatching's predictions; our Sparse Blossom gives PyMatching's
  matching weight on every compared shot.
* **BP+UF ties correlated PyMatching at p = 0.3–0.4%** (d = 7: 3.5 per 10,000 rounds both) and beats plain
  PyMatching by 1.8×; at p = 0.1% correlated PyMatching is 1.8× better.
* **Lookup table is the most accurate decoder at d = 3** (2.2e-4 vs 2.7e-4 PyMatching at p = 0.1%).
* **Our BP+OSD beats ldpc's BP+OSD** (6.1e-4 vs 1.0e-3 at d = 5, p = 0.3%; library point has 15 errors).
* **Union-Find on 12 threads matches PyMatching's throughput at d = 7** (1.83 vs 1.89 µs/shot).
* **Local BP+UF is 3–5× faster than global BP+UF on one thread, 7–9× on 12**, same accuracy from d = 7 up.
* **Results reproduce** (Claude 2: 60/60 points within intervals).

## 7. Fault tolerance

* Every single fault is corrected by all our decoders except BP alone (d = 3 and 5). Correlated PyMatching fails a
  few single faults at d = 3.
* 2-fault failures at d = 5: 0 for UF, UF weighted, MWPM, Sparse Blossom, local BP+UF; 0.07–0.3% for BP+UF and
  BP+OSD; 0.7% greedy+2-opt; 2.1% greedy.
* Greedy (+2-opt) keeps failing small bunched fault clusters at every d (needs 3–5 pairs re-matched at once), so it
  doesn't improve from d = 5 to 7 (Claude 2).
